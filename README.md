# 🌸 SheRise AI

> **Empowering women with personalized opportunities, skills, government schemes, and safety support — powered by AI.**

SheRise AI is an AI-powered women's empowerment platform that helps users discover relevant **jobs, courses, skill-development opportunities, government schemes, and personalized career action plans** based on their individual profile.

The platform also provides an integrated **SOS/Safety system** designed to quickly initiate emergency assistance through predefined safety workflows.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Women often face difficulty finding the right combination of:

- Employment opportunities
- Skill-development programs
- Relevant courses
- Government schemes
- Career guidance
- Emergency safety resources

Existing platforms generally provide information in isolation.

SheRise AI brings these capabilities together into a single personalized platform.

Instead of making users search through thousands of opportunities manually, SheRise analyzes their profile and recommends the most relevant options.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

SheRise AI uses a combination of:

- 👤 Personalized user profiles
- 🤖 Generative AI
- 📊 Deterministic recommendation scoring
- 💼 Job datasets
- 🎓 Course recommendations
- 🏛️ Government scheme information
- 🆘 SOS/Safety workflows
- 🔄 n8n automation
- 🌐 Responsive web interface

The system follows a hybrid approach:

```text
User Profile
     ↓

     ↓
Dataset Filtering
     ↓

     ↓
Relevant Candidates

     ↓
