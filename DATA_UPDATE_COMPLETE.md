## 3️⃣ GOVERNMENT SCHEMES PAGE - 10 Real Schemes

### Updated Government Schemes:

#### Women-Focused Schemes:
1. **Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana**
   - Benefit: ₹1,500/month cash transfer
   - Eligibility: Married women 21-65 years, family income ≤ ₹2.5L
   - Category: Women

2. **Lek Ladki Yojana**
   - Benefit: ₹46,000 at age 18 for higher education
   - Eligibility: Girls born from April 2021+, family income ≤ ₹1L
   - Category: Women

3. **Stand Up India**
   - Benefit: ₹10L to ₹1Cr collateral-free loans
   - Eligibility: Women, SC/ST entrepreneurs, 18-65 years
   - Category: Women

4. **PM Mudra Yojana**
   - Benefit: ₹50K to ₹10L without collateral
   - Eligibility: Individuals starting micro businesses
   - Category: Women

5. **Savitribai Phule Scholarship**
   - Benefit: ₹4K to ₹25K per annum
   - Eligibility: Girls from low-income families, 60%+ marks
   - Category: Women

#### General Schemes:
6. **PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)**
   - Benefit: Free skill training, stipend, placement
   - Eligibility: Youth 15-45 years, dropouts, unemployed
   - Category: General

7. **MAHASWAYAM Employment Portal**
   - Benefit: Job listings, career guidance, placement
   - Eligibility: All registered Maharashtra job seekers
   - Category: General

#### Reserved Category Schemes:
8. **National Fellowship for SC/ST**
   - Benefit: Monthly stipend + tuition up to ₹4L
   - Eligibility: SC/ST pursuing MA, MSc, MTech
   - Category: SC

9. **Post Matric Scholarship**
   - Benefit: Tuition fees + monthly stipend
   - Eligibility: EWS/OBC after 12th pass
   - Category: EWS

10. **EBC Scholarship Scheme**
    - Benefit: Tuition reimbursement + ₹500-₹1K monthly
    - Eligibility: EBC category, family income ≤ ₹1L/year
    - Category: EWS

**Scheme Card Improvements:**
- ✅ Displayed benefit amount prominently
- ✅ Clear eligibility criteria
- ✅ Category tags (Women, SC, EWS, General)
- ✅ Direct government scheme names
